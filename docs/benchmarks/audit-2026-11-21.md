### 4.2 In-Process Embedded Session (`std/db/yazabase_embedded`, `-O2`) & YBIF C Direct

- **1,000 Scoped Primary-Key `SELECT`s:** **`9,995.2 ops/sec`** (`p50 = 0.099 ms`, `p95 = 0.106 ms`, `p99 = 0.117 ms`)
- **500 Batched Transactional `INSERT`s (`10 × 50`, scoped):** **`882.7 rows/sec`** (`566.47 ms` total, **42.7×** higher throughput than standalone commits)
- **500 Standalone Durable `INSERT`s (`F_FULLFSYNC`, scoped):** **`20.7 tx/sec`** (`p50 = 50.342 ms`, `p95 = 71.333 ms`, `p99 = 76.543 ms`)
- **Peak RSS (scoped loop):** **`37.3 MiB`** (`39,157,760 B` max RSS, **`31.1 MiB`** footprint — **14.8× lower** than unscoped)
- **Direct Binary YBIF vs JSON (`yazabase_result_encode_ybif`, 20k × 16 rows):** **`7.723 ms`** (`2.59M ops/s`, `896 B`) vs `16.419 ms` (`1.22M ops/s`, `1,188 B`) — **2.13× faster** and **24.6% smaller**
